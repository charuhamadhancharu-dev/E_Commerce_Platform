1. Introduction
1.1 Purpose
This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the functional and non-functional requirements of ShopNext, an online retail platform. It is derived from the supplied UI design board, which covers the landing page, product listing, product details, shopping cart, checkout, authentication, user dashboard, admin dashboard and a shared component library. The document is intended for product owners, designers, developers, QA engineers and project managers, and serves as the baseline for design, development, testing and acceptance.
1.2 Scope
ShopNext enables customers to discover, evaluate and purchase products across categories such as Electronics, Fashion, Home & Living, Beauty, Sports and Toys & Games. It provides a customer-facing storefront and account area, and an administrative console for managing products, orders, users, analytics and marketing.
In scope:
Customer storefront: browsing, search, filtering, product detail, wishlist, cart, checkout and order tracking
Authentication and account management (email/password and social login)
User dashboard with order history, wishlist, address and settings management
Admin dashboard with KPIs, revenue analytics, product, order and user management and marketing tools
Reusable UI component library with light/dark theme support
Out of scope (for this release):
Native mobile applications (the web application shall be responsive)
Multi-vendor marketplace and seller onboarding
Warehouse management and logistics carrier integration beyond shipment status updates
Customer support chat and ticketing

1.5 Document Conventions
Requirements are uniquely identified (e.g., FR-CART-03). The keyword "shall" denotes a mandatory requirement. Priority is rated High (H), Medium (M) or Low (L). H means required for launch; M means important but may be deferred; L means desirable.
2. Overall Description
2.1 Product Perspective
ShopNext is a new, standalone web platform composed of a responsive single-page storefront, a backend REST/GraphQL API, a relational database, and integrations with third-party payment gateways, identity providers, email/SMS services and shipping providers. The UI follows a modern dark theme with indigo/violet accents, glass-style cards and a persistent navigation bar, and the design includes a theme toggle.
2.2 Product Functions (Summary)
Landing page with hero banner, value propositions (free shipping, easy returns, secure payment) and top categories
Product listing with filters (category, price range, colour, brand), sorting, grid/list view and pagination
Product details with image gallery, ratings, variants, pricing, discounts, add-to-cart and buy-now
Shopping cart with quantity management, order summary and recommendations
Three-step checkout: shipping, payment, review, with multiple payment methods
Login/registration with social sign-in and password recovery
User dashboard: order statistics, recent orders, wishlist, addresses, settings and promotional offers
Admin dashboard: revenue, orders, active users, conversion rate, top products, and CRUD modules

2.4 Operating Environment
Browsers: latest two versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari; mobile Safari and Chrome
Devices: desktop, tablet and mobile (responsive breakpoints: 360, 768, 1024, 1440 px)
Server: cloud-hosted (containerised) Linux environment with managed relational database and object storage
2.5 Design and Implementation Constraints
The UI shall conform to the provided design: dark theme default, component library and navigation patterns
Payment card data shall never be stored on ShopNext servers; a PCI-DSS compliant gateway shall be used
Payment methods shall support the Indian market (UPI) as well as cards, PayPal and COD, as depicted in the checkout screen
Currency display is USD in the mock-ups; multi-currency support shall be configurable
2.6 Assumptions and Dependencies
Third-party services (payment gateway, OAuth providers, email/SMS, shipping) are available and contracted
Product content (images, descriptions, specifications) is supplied by the business
Tax rules are configurable by region; the mock-up shows a tax line in the cart and order summary
Free shipping threshold is $50 as shown on the landing page and is configurable

4. External Interface Requirements
4.1 User Interfaces
Visual style: dark theme with deep navy/black backgrounds, indigo-violet gradient primary buttons, rounded cards, subtle glow and glass effects
Typography: a clean sans-serif family with clear hierarchy (hero, headings, body, captions)
Layout: 12-column responsive grid; sidebars collapse on smaller screens
Status colours: green for success/delivered, amber for pending/warnings, red for errors
All interactive elements shall have hover, focus, active and disabled states
4.2 Hardware Interfaces
No direct hardware interfaces. The application shall run in standard web browsers on user devices and support touch input.

5.2 Security



ID


Requirement


NFR-SEC-01


Passwords shall be hashed using bcrypt/Argon2 with salt; never stored in plain text.


NFR-SEC-02


The system shall protect against OWASP Top 10 risks including XSS, CSRF, SQL injection and broken access control.


NFR-SEC-03


Role-based access control shall be enforced server-side for all admin functions.

5.3 Reliability and Availability
The platform shall target 99.9% monthly uptime excluding planned maintenance
Automated daily database backups with 30-day retention; RPO of 15 minutes or less and RTO of 4 hours or less
Graceful degradation when third-party services are unavailable
5.4 Usability and Accessibility
The UI shall conform to WCAG 2.2 Level AA, including keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels and sufficient colour contrast in both dark and light themes
The checkout shall be completable in three steps with clear inline error messages
The UI shall be fully responsive from 360 px width upward
5.5 Maintainability and Portability
Code shall follow a modular architecture with automated tests (unit coverage of 80% or more for business logic)
CI/CD pipeline with automated build, test and deployment
Configuration (currency, tax, shipping, payment methods) shall be changeable without code deployment
5.6 Localisation
The system shall support multiple languages and currencies, locale-specific date/number formats and region-specific payment methods (e.g., UPI and COD).
5.7 Legal and Compliance
The platform shall comply with applicable data protection laws (e.g., India's DPDP Act, GDPR where applicable), consumer protection and e-commerce regulations, and shall provide cookie consent, privacy policy, terms of service and return policy pages.

6.2 Business Rules
Order total = Subtotal + Shipping + Tax - Discount
Discount percentage = (Original Price - Sale Price) / Original Price x 100, rounded to the nearest whole number
Free shipping applies when the subtotal is above the configured threshold (default $50)
Orders may be cancelled by customers only before the Shipped status
Return window defaults to 30 days from delivery
Stock shall be reserved at order placement and released if payment fails or the order is cancelled

Appendix B: Open Issues and Design Observations
The following inconsistencies were noted in the UI mock-ups and should be resolved with the design team before development:
Product Details shows $349 against $390 with a "13% OFF" badge, but the actual discount is about 10.5%. The system shall calculate the badge automatically (FR-PDP-04).
The Checkout order summary shows the product name "Sony WH-1000XM5 Wireless Headphones" ($349) with a smartwatch image, while the cart contains three different items. Product images and names must be bound to the same data source.
The Cart screen shows the Nike product subtitle as "Running Soees" (typo); UI copy should be proofread.
The Cart tax of $70.16 on a subtotal of $877 (about 8%) implies a fixed tax rate; the tax rules (inclusive vs exclusive, regional rates) must be confirmed.
Checkout lists UPI and COD alongside PayPal and Visa, which implies India and international markets; supported regions and currencies need confirmation.
Admin dashboard Y-axis labels on the revenue chart repeat ("16k" appears twice), which should be corrected in the final design.
No UI is shown for order confirmation, order detail, returns/refunds, coupon entry, search results, 404/error pages, or the admin Products/Orders/Users list screens. These are included as recommended requirements and need design.
